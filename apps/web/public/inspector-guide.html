How the System Works

  • 📋 The inspection is created by the Service Advisor from a customer lead. You (the inspector) will receive the assigned inspection and work through it step-by-step.
  • 🔒 Each step is locked until the previous step is completed. The system will block you from advancing if mandatory fields are missing.
  • 💾 Your progress is auto-saved as a draft continuously. You can close and reopen the page — your work will be preserved.
  • 🤖 The system has an AI assistant that suggests related parts, asks guided questions, and flags potential issues based on the vehicle and findings.
1

Collect Car

Gate / Reception
ℹ️ This step is completed before the car enters the workshop bay. You review the media uploaded at the time the car was received (recovery pickup or walk-in check-in).

What You Will See

  • 🚗 Source Type — Shows whether the car came in via Recovery Pickup or Walk-in Check-in.
  • 📸 Source Media — Photos and video uploaded at the time of car collection (Front, Rear, Right, Left, 360 Video).
  • 🔍 Pre-inspection Form — Customer-reported issues, complaints, and notes submitted before the car arrived.

Your Actions

  • ✅ Compare the actual car in front of you with the source media photos.
  • ⚠️ Select "Yes, there is a difference" if the car condition does not match the source photos. Describe what is different.
  • ✔️ Select "No difference" if the car matches exactly.
  • ✅ Click "Mark Collect Car Complete" to finish Step 1 and unlock Step 2.
⚠️ You cannot skip this step. If there is a difference between the media and the actual car, you must document it before proceeding. This protects the workshop against liability claims.
2

Start Inspection

Bay / Workshop
ℹ️ This step officially starts the inspection clock. The system records the timestamp when you click Start. Always click Start when you physically begin working on the car.

Your Actions

  • ✅ Review the confirmation message that Step 1 (Collect Car) is complete.
  • ▶️ Click the "Start Inspection" button to record the start time and move to Step 3.
3

Checks & Notes

Visual + Initial Checks
ℹ️ This step covers two tasks: (1) verifying the car photos/video uploaded at check-in, and (2) documenting any visible damages or issues with supporting photos.

Part A — Car Media Verification

You will see all check-in photos and the 360° video. For each one, you must either Verify or Reject it.

  • ✅ Verify — The photo/video is clear, correct angle, and matches the car. Click "Verify" to approve it.
  • ❌ Reject — The photo is blurry, wrong angle, or does not show the car clearly. Click "Reject", select a reject reason, add a note, and upload a replacement photo.
Reject Reasons
Blur · Wrong angle · Not same car · Blocked view · Poor lighting
Required
All rejected media must have a replacement upload before Step 4 unlocks

Part B — Issues / Damage Notes

Document any scratches, dents, rust, broken parts, or other visible damage found during your initial walk-around.

  • ➕ Click "+ Add Note" to open the damage entry form.
  • ✍️ Write a clear description of the damage (e.g. "Deep scratch on rear bumper, left side").
  • 📷 Upload a photo of the damage. Tap "Add Note" to save it.
  • 🔁 Repeat for each separate damage point. You can add as many notes as needed.
💡 Document every damage note here — even minor ones. These notes are included in the final inspection report and protect both the customer and the workshop.

Using the VIN Check Feature

  • 1️⃣ Type or scan the VIN into the VIN field. VIN must be exactly 17 characters.
  • 2️⃣ Click "Check VIN". The system will look up the vehicle details automatically.
  • 3️⃣ If multiple matches are found, a dropdown will appear — select the correct vehicle variant.
  • ✅ The fields for Make, Model, and Year will be auto-filled. The VIN also unlocks the Parts Catalog in Step 5.
⚠️ All four required fields (VIN, Plate, Mileage, both Tyre sizes) must be completed before you can advance to Step 5. Double-check the mileage matches the odometer in the car.
5

Inspection Findings & Parts

Core Inspection Work
ℹ️ This is the main inspection step where you document all required parts and findings. There are two sections: Inspection Checks (quick system checks with photos) and Findings / Parts Needed (detailed line items).

Part A — Inspection Checks (Oil · Battery · Tyre · OBD)

Four quick system checks that must be documented with a status and supporting photos.

Check Status Options What to Do
Oil Check OK  Issue  N/A Inspect oil level and color. Upload dipstick or engine bay photo.
Battery Check OK  Issue  N/A Check battery terminals and voltage. Upload battery photo.
Tyre Check OK  Issue  N/A Check tread depth and pressure on all 4 tyres. Upload tyre photo.
OBD Check OK  Issue  N/A Connect OBD scanner. Upload screenshot of fault codes or "no faults" screen.

If any check shows Issue, a description field will appear — describe the problem in detail.

AI Line Item Assistant

The blue banner at the top of the Findings section shows AI-generated guidance based on the vehicle and parts already added.

  • ❓ Questions — The AI asks diagnostic questions to help you think of parts you might have missed (e.g. "Have you checked the brake fluid level?").
  • 💡 Suggestions — The AI recommends related parts commonly replaced alongside the ones you've already added.
💡 Read the AI questions before finalizing the inspection — they often catch parts that are easy to overlook during a busy day.

Click "+ Add Line Item" (top right of Findings section, or bottom of the list) to open the Parts Catalog popup.

  • 1️⃣ EPC field — Auto-filled from the VIN lookup. If empty, enter the brand code manually (e.g. audi_vw, toyota). Then click Reload to load the catalog.
  • 2️⃣ Level 1 — Main Category — Select the system area (e.g. Engine, Transmission, Brakes, Body).
  • 3️⃣ Level 2 / 3 / 4 — Drill down through sub-categories until the parts list appears. Some vehicles have 2 levels; others have up to 4.
  • 4️⃣ Select parts — Tick the checkboxes next to each part you need. Use Search to filter by part name or number. Use Select All to select all visible parts.
  • 5️⃣ Click "Add Selected Parts". The modal closes and the parts appear as new line items in the Findings table.
  • 🔁 Repeat for each system/category. Open the modal again and select a different Level 1 category to add more parts.

Part C — Manual Entry (When No Catalog is Available)

If the system cannot find a VIN catalog for this vehicle, an amber "Manual Inspection Entry" panel will automatically appear.

  • 1️⃣ Click "+ Add Manual Entry" to expand the form.
  • 2️⃣ Select the System / Category (Engine, Gearbox, Brakes, Suspension, Electrical, Body, Other).
  • 3️⃣ Select the Severity (Safety Risk, Mandatory, Recommended, Optional).
  • 4️⃣ In "What did you observe?" — describe what you physically saw or heard (e.g. "Worn brake pads, metal-on-metal grinding when braking").
  • 5️⃣ Enter the Part Name and Part Number if known. Part number can be left blank — the vendor will confirm it.
  • 6️⃣ Set the Quantity (default is 1). Click "Add to Findings".

Part D — Working with Line Items (Findings Table)

Each part added from the catalog or manual entry appears as a row in the Findings table. Expand each row to complete the details.

  • 🔽 Expand a row — Click the row or the expand button to open its full detail panel.
  • 🔢 Part Name / Number / Qty — Edit the auto-filled fields if needed. Part name comes from the catalog; edit it if incorrect.
  • ⚡ Severity / Status — Set to: Safety Risk Mandatory Recommended Optional
  • 🤖 AI Questions — Answer the per-part AI questions (Yes / No / N/A). These help the AI generate better suggestions for related parts.
  • 🖼️ Part Diagram — If available, a diagram image is shown inline. If not, a "Search Diagram" Google Images link is shown for that part number.
  • ✍️ Inspector Note — Add any additional observation about this specific part (optional but helpful for vendors).
  • 💾 Save the row — Click "Save" on each row, or use "Save All Draft" at the top to save all unsaved rows at once.
  • 🗑️ Remove a row — Click the remove/delete button on the row to delete an incorrectly added part.

Severity Level Guide

Level Meaning Examples
Safety Risk Vehicle is unsafe to drive. Repair is urgent and cannot be delayed. Failed brakes, no steering fluid, bald tyres, cracked windshield
Mandatory Must be repaired. Vehicle may break down without this fix. Engine misfires, transmission slipping, coolant leak
Recommended Should be done soon to prevent bigger problems. Worn brake pads (30%), dirty filters, cracked belts
Optional Nice to fix but not urgent. Customer can decide. Cosmetic scratches, minor interior trim, cabin air filter

AI Smart Fault Suggestions

After you add a line item from the catalog, the AI analyzes the category and vehicle, then suggests other parts commonly affected in the same system.

  • 💡 Suggestions appear below the Findings table header. Each suggestion has a label and reason explaining why this part may also need attention.
  • ➕ Click "Add" next to a suggestion to instantly add it as a new line item. The AI reason becomes the Inspector Note for that row.
  • 🔄 Suggestions update automatically each time you add or save a new line item.
💡 Use "Save All Draft" regularly as you work. This ensures nothing is lost if the page is accidentally refreshed.
6

Inspector Remarks & Final Report

Final Step
ℹ️ This is the final step. You write your overall inspection remarks, then generate and download the PDF inspection report for the customer and service advisor.

Inspector Remarks

  • ✍️ Write a final summary note in the remarks field. Cover the overall condition of the vehicle, your main concerns, and any special observations.
  • 📝 Example: "Vehicle is in generally good condition. Main concern is safety — the front brake pads are worn to metal and must be replaced immediately. The engine oil is overdue for a change. Recommend full fluid service alongside brake work."

Inspection Report

The system automatically compiles all inspection data into a professional PDF report. Click "Download Inspection Report (PDF)" to generate and save it.

Report Includes
Workshop logo · Vehicle details · Customer info · All findings with severity · Inspector remarks · Issue photos · Process check results
Health Score
Auto-calculated score (0–100) based on severity of all findings and system category weights
  • 📄 The report is formatted for A4 print and can be emailed to the customer or presented on screen.
  • ✅ After completing remarks and downloading the report, the inspection is marked as complete and becomes read-only.

Quick Reference

Tips & Reminders

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ✗Do not skip the VIN Check — the parts catalog will not load without a valid VIN.
  • ✗Do not set all parts to "Recommended" — use the correct severity level for each finding.
  • ✗Do not forget to Save All Draft before moving to Step 6.
  • ✗Do not reject a media photo without uploading a replacement — Step 4 will remain locked.
  • ✗Do not leave the Part Number blank for Safety Risk items — vendors need it to source parts quickly.

Best Practices

  • ✓Take clear, well-lit photos from multiple angles for each damage note.
  • ✓Add parts in logical groups — all brake parts together, all engine parts together.
  • ✓Review AI suggestions after adding each group of parts.
  • ✓Write observation notes on each line item — this helps vendors price accurately.
  • ✓Check the OBD for fault codes even if the customer didn't mention warning lights.
