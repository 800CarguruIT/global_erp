# Vendor Portal Operations

## Car-Based Parts Quotation Workflow Guide

## Who Should Read This?

- Vendor Portal Users
- Vendor Sales and Parts Teams
- Procurement Officers
- Company Operations Managers

## 1. What Is This Page Used For?

Vendor Portal Operations allows vendors to receive part requirements linked to vehicle service cases, submit quotations, and track the quote decision lifecycle.

The vendor process is centered on:

- Viewing cars/service requests
- Reviewing required parts
- Submitting quotes
- Following approval or rejection outcomes

## 2. Workflow Start Point

The workflow starts when company procurement publishes inquiry items to vendors.

Typical vendor-side path:

Vendor Portal -> Cars/Requests -> Required Parts -> Submit Quote

Each inquiry is linked to operational context so vendors can quote accurately.

## 3. Step-by-Step Vendor Flow

### Step 1: Open Cars or Service Requests

- Login to Vendor Portal
- Open `Cars` or `Service Requests` list (as configured)
- Select the target vehicle/request

Vendor should review:

- Car details (make/model/year if shown)
- Request reference
- Branch/location
- Required delivery timeline

### Step 2: Review Required Parts

- Open `Required Parts` for selected request
- Review each item specification:
  - Part name/code
  - Quantity
  - Brand/spec notes
  - Delivery requirement

Before quoting, confirm stock availability and realistic lead time.

### Step 3: Prepare Quote

For each required line item, provide:

- Unit price
- Available quantity
- Delivery lead time
- Warranty/quality notes (if applicable)
- Commercial terms (if requested)

### Step 4: Submit Quote

- Save quote draft
- Validate all mandatory fields
- Submit quote

After submit:

- Quote reference is generated
- Procurement team receives the quote for comparison
- Status moves from draft to submitted state

## 4. Quote Status Flow (Vendor View)

Typical statuses visible to vendor:

- Draft
- Submitted
- Under Review
- Approved
- Rejected
- Revision Requested
- Converted to PO

If revision is requested, vendor should update pricing or terms and resubmit.

## 5. After Quote Approval

When quote is approved:

- Procurement creates purchase order (PO)
- Vendor receives PO details
- Vendor confirms fulfillment timeline
- Delivery process starts

Vendor should ensure PO item, quantity, and price match approved quote.

## 6. Delivery and Receiving Relationship

After dispatch by vendor:

- Company receives parts against PO
- Goods receipt is recorded
- Any short/damaged delivery is logged

Vendors should respond quickly to discrepancy cases to avoid job delays.

## 7. Operational Rules and Restrictions

- Do not quote items without confirming availability
- Do not submit duplicate quotes for same request unless revision is required
- Do not change price after approval without formal revision
- Keep quote and PO references consistent
- Submit on time to avoid SLA breach and missed opportunities

## 8. Validation Checklist

Before submitting quote, confirm:

- Correct request/car selected
- All required parts reviewed
- Prices and lead times are accurate
- Terms are complete
- Quote status is `Submitted`

After decision, confirm:

- Approved quotes are mapped to PO
- Rejected/revision notes are reviewed
- Delivery commitments are tracked

## Executive Summary

Vendor Portal Operations flow:

Open Car/Request -> Review Required Parts -> Prepare Quote -> Submit -> Review Outcome -> PO/Delivery

This process keeps procurement transparent, traceable, and aligned with automotive service timelines.
