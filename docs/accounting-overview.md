# Accounts Module

## End-to-End Accounting Operations Guide

## Who Should Read This?

- Finance Managers
- Accountants and Finance Officers
- Billing and Collections Teams
- Operations Managers reviewing financial impact
- Company Administrators

## 1. What Is the Accounts Module?

The Accounts module is where operational activity is converted into controlled financial records for reporting and compliance.

It connects:

- Service revenue and customer billing
- Procurement and vendor liabilities
- Inventory cost movements
- Payments and settlements
- Journals and period-end closing

This module ensures all financial impacts are posted, traceable, and auditable.

## 2. Core Accounting Areas

- Chart of Accounts
- Accounts Receivable (AR)
- Accounts Payable (AP)
- Journals and Adjustments
- Cost Posting from Inventory/Operations
- Reconciliation
- Financial Statements and Management Reporting

## 3. End-to-End Financial Flow

### Step 1: Operational Transaction

- Job completion and approved billing scope
- Procurement receipts and vendor invoices
- Inventory movements affecting cost

### Step 2: Document Creation

- Customer invoice generated
- Vendor bill or payable recorded
- Supporting references linked (job, PO, receipt)

### Step 3: Accounting Posting

- Revenue, cost, receivable, and payable entries posted
- Tax and charges applied per configuration

### Step 4: Payment and Settlement

- Customer payment posted against receivable
- Vendor payment posted against payable
- Partials/advances matched where applicable

### Step 5: Reconciliation and Review

- AR/AP aging reviewed
- Suspense and unmatched entries resolved
- Inventory-cost and COGS relationships validated

### Step 6: Period Close

- Final validation checks completed
- Accounting period locked
- Financial reports published

## 4. Posting Status Flow (Typical)

Common document and posting statuses:

- Draft
- Pending Approval
- Approved
- Posted
- Partially Settled
- Settled
- Reversed
- Closed

Use consistent status transitions to keep reporting accurate.

## 5. Accounts Receivable (Customer Side)

AR process typically includes:

- Generate invoice from approved service scope
- Validate billable lines and tax
- Send invoice
- Record payment receipt
- Reconcile partial/full settlement

Controls:

- Do not post invoice without approved operational scope
- Do not allow silent edits after posting
- Use credit notes/reversal flow for corrections

## 6. Accounts Payable (Vendor Side)

AP process typically includes:

- Capture vendor bill against PO/receipt
- Validate quantity, price, and tax
- Approve payable
- Post liability
- Process payment and settlement

Controls:

- Match payable against PO and goods receipt
- Block duplicate vendor bills
- Enforce approval for high-value disbursements

## 7. Journals and Adjustments

Manual journals should be controlled and documented.

Best practice:

- Limit posting rights to authorized finance users
- Require reason and supporting reference
- Route high-impact entries for approval
- Keep full audit trail for edits/reversals

## 8. Reconciliation Process

Regular reconciliation should cover:

- Bank reconciliation
- AR invoice vs collection matching
- AP bill vs payment matching
- Inventory valuation vs GL control accounts
- Suspense and temporary account clearing

## 9. Period-End Closing Checklist

- Confirm all required invoices are posted
- Reconcile AR and AP balances
- Validate inventory-related cost postings
- Review manual journals and exceptions
- Confirm branch/company rollups
- Lock period after approval
- Publish management and statutory reports

## 10. Controls and Restrictions

Critical controls:

- Role-based access for posting and approvals
- Period lock after close
- Reversal flow instead of direct deletion of posted records
- Approval thresholds for high-value transactions
- Audit trail retention for all financial events

## 11. KPIs and Reports

Recommended outputs:

- Profit and Loss by period/branch
- Balance Sheet
- Cashflow summary
- AR aging and collection performance
- AP aging and payment performance
- Gross margin by service category

## Executive Summary

The Accounts module converts operations into reliable financial results.

Flow:

Operations -> Invoice/Bill -> Posting -> Payment/Settlement -> Reconciliation -> Period Close -> Reporting

It is the financial control backbone for accurate profitability, compliance, and management visibility in the automotive ERP platform.
