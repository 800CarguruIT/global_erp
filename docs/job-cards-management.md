# Job Cards Management

## Job Card Creation, Assignment, Start & Completion Guide

## Who Should Read This?

- Service Advisors
- Workshop Supervisors
- Technicians
- Operations Managers
- Company Administrators

## 1. What Is Job Cards Management?

Job Cards Management is the process of converting approved service scope into executable workshop tasks.

A job card defines:

- What work will be done
- Who will do it
- What parts/labor are required
- When execution starts and ends

It is the operational control record for service execution.

## 2. Where Job Card Creation Starts

Job card creation should start from an approved estimate linked to an active lead and car.

Typical path:

Car Dashboard -> Active Lead -> Approved Estimate -> Create Job Card

Do not create major execution work without approved scope.

## 3. Creating a Job Card

### Step-by-step

1. Open the active service case from car dashboard
2. Open approved estimate
3. Click `Create Job Card`
4. Confirm included tasks and line items
5. Set job priority and expected timelines
6. Save

After save:

- Job card reference is generated
- Job enters workshop queue
- Execution tracking becomes active

## 4. Assigning a Job Card

Assignment can be done at creation or from job card detail.

Typical assignment fields:

- Lead technician
- Supporting technicians (if applicable)
- Bay/workstation
- Supervisor
- Planned start date/time

Assignment rules:

- Assignee must have valid role and branch access
- Assigned team should match job type
- Reassignment should be logged for traceability

## 5. Job Card Process Flow (Start to Complete)

### Step 1: Ready for Start

- Job is created and assigned
- Required approvals are complete
- Parts readiness is verified (or flagged as waiting)

### Step 2: Start Job

- Technician/supervisor clicks `Start`
- Actual start time is captured
- Job status moves to `In Progress`

### Step 3: Execute Tasks

- Technicians perform assigned tasks
- Labor entries and notes are recorded
- Parts consumption is tracked
- Progress status is updated continuously

### Step 4: Work Completion

- All approved tasks marked complete
- Quality/supervisor review performed
- Exceptions/rework resolved

### Step 5: Complete Job Card

- Supervisor or authorized user clicks `Complete`
- Job is locked for execution edits (policy-based)
- Case becomes ready for invoicing/delivery process

## 6. Job Card Status Flow (Typical)

Common statuses:

- Draft
- Assigned
- Ready to Start
- In Progress
- Waiting for Parts
- On Hold
- Completed
- Closed

Use consistent status transitions for clean reporting and SLA tracking.

## 7. Conditions and Restrictions

Operational controls typically include:

- Job cannot start without assignment
- Job should not start if required approvals are missing
- Completion requires all mandatory tasks done
- Completed job cards should be restricted from uncontrolled edits
- Delete action should be blocked for executed job cards
- Status changes must be auditable

These controls protect execution quality and billing integrity.

## 8. Verification in Operations Dashboard

After creation/assignment/start/completion, verify in operations dashboard:

- Job appears in correct branch queue
- Assigned technician/team is correct
- Current status is accurate
- Aging and SLA indicators are correct (if enabled)
- Completed jobs move out of active execution queue

## 9. Related Process After Completion

After job card completion:

- Final billable scope is validated
- Invoice process starts
- Vehicle handover (Car Out / Delivered) is completed
- Operational and financial reports update

Job card completion is the main handoff point to billing and delivery.

## 10. Validation Checklist

After managing a job card, confirm:

- Job linked to correct lead, car, and estimate
- Assignment is correct and visible
- Start and completion timestamps are recorded
- Task and parts consumption are complete
- Final status is correct
- Audit trail is available

## Executive Summary

Job Cards Management controls workshop execution from approved scope to final completion.

Flow:

Approved Estimate -> Create Job Card -> Assign -> Start -> Execute -> Complete -> Invoice/Delivery

It ensures service execution is controlled, trackable, and ready for accurate billing and handover.
