# Global ERP System

## Simple Business Overview

## 1. What Is Global ERP?

Global ERP is a complete business management system that helps organizations manage their daily operations from one unified platform.

It connects:

- Headquarters (Global level)
- Company management
- Branch operations
- Vendors and workshop partners

Instead of using separate systems for sales, inventory, procurement, and accounting, everything is managed in one place.

Think of it as the central nervous system of the business - all departments work through it, and all data lives in one trusted source.

## 2. Why Does This System Exist?

Global ERP was built to solve common business problems:

### 1. One Source of Truth

All departments work from the same data.
No duplicate spreadsheets. No disconnected systems.

### 2. Standardized Process

Every job follows the same structured flow - from customer lead to final invoice.

### 3. Controlled Access

Each user only sees and does what they are allowed to, depending on their role and location.

### 4. Vendor Collaboration

Suppliers and workshop partners can interact directly with the system for quotes, orders, and deliveries.

## 3. Who Uses Global ERP?

The system is structured into different levels of access.

### Global Level (Head Office / Operations Control)

Used for:

- Managing companies
- Defining system policies
- Managing global users and permissions
- Monitoring performance across all companies

This level controls the entire platform.

### Company Level (Business Management)

Used for:

- Managing branches
- Managing customers and vendors
- Viewing reports
- Monitoring financial performance
- Overseeing daily operations

This is where business leadership operates.

### Branch Level (Operational Execution)

Used for:

- Creating jobs
- Performing inspections
- Managing inventory
- Executing customer work
- Recording daily activities

This is where actual operational work happens.

### Vendor / Partner Level

Used by:

- Suppliers
- Workshop partners

They can:

- Respond to inquiries
- Submit quotes
- Confirm deliveries
- Update order status

This keeps procurement organized and transparent.

## 4. What Business Areas Does It Cover?

Global ERP combines several business functions into one system:

### 1. User & Access Management

Controls who can do what in the system.

### 2. Leads / CRM

Tracks potential customers and business opportunities.

### 3. Workshop & Job Management

Manages inspections, job cards, and execution of work.

### 4. Procurement

Handles supplier communication, purchase orders, and approvals.

### 5. Inventory Management

Tracks stock levels, transfers, and usage across branches.

### 6. Billing & Accounting

Creates invoices and records financial transactions.

### 7. Reporting & Analytics

Provides performance reports and operational insights.

### 8. Mobile Support

Allows field teams and workshop users to operate through mobile devices.

## 5. How Does Work Flow Through the System?

Here is a typical example of how a job moves through Global ERP:

### Step 1: A Lead Is Created

A potential customer opportunity is recorded.

### Step 2: Inspection Is Performed

Technical team assesses the job requirements.

### Step 3: Job Planning

Work is defined and parts are identified.

### Step 4: Procurement (If Parts Are Needed)

Suppliers are contacted and purchase orders are created.

### Step 5: Inventory Handling

Parts are received and recorded into stock.

### Step 6: Work Execution

The job is completed by the branch team.

### Step 7: Billing

Customer invoice is generated.

### Step 8: Accounting & Reporting

Financial entries are automatically recorded for reporting.

Everything is connected and traceable.

## 7. How Is Security Handled?

Security is built into the system:

- Access is controlled by role and level (global/company/branch).
- Every important action can be audited.
- Sensitive operations require proper authorization.
- External integrations are controlled and secure.

This ensures operational control and accountability.

## 8. Why This System Is Important for the Business

Global ERP enables:

- Operational visibility across branches
- Controlled procurement processes
- Accurate inventory tracking
- Real-time financial reporting
- Scalable multi-company management
- Reduced operational errors
- Clear accountability

It allows leadership to monitor performance while enabling branches to execute efficiently.

## 9. Key Terms Explained (Simple Glossary)

### Lead

A potential customer opportunity.

### Inspection

A technical evaluation before deciding what work is needed.

### Job Card

The structured work order used to execute a job.

### PO (Purchase Order)

An official order sent to a supplier for parts or services.

### GRN (Goods Receipt Note)

Confirmation that ordered items have been received.

### Inventory Movement

Any stock entering, leaving, or transferring between locations.

## Final Summary

Global ERP is a centralized operations and financial management system that connects:

- Management
- Branches
- Suppliers
- Finance teams

It standardizes how work flows from customer opportunity to final accounting, while maintaining security, traceability, and operational control.

It is designed to support business growth with structure, visibility, and accountability.
