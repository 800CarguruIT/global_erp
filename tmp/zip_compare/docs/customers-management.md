# Customers Management

## Customer Setup & Access Control Guide

---

## Who Should Read This?

* Company Administrators
* Sales and Operations Managers
* Customer Service Supervisors
* Security or IT Administrators supporting access

---

# 1. What Is Customers Management?

Customers Management is the area where company-level teams manage customer records used by sales, operations, jobs, and billing.

Primary page path:

`/company/[companyId]/customers`

Each customer profile is a controlled master record that supports operational workflows and reporting.

---

# 2. What Can You Do on This Page?

Through Customers Management, you can:

* View all customers
* Create new customer profiles
* Edit existing customer information
* Activate or deactivate customer records
* Maintain clean customer data for operations
* Open Customer Dashboard for profile-level activity and insights

This ensures teams use one reliable customer source across modules.

---

# 2.1 Customer Dashboard

From the customer list, administrators or authorized users can open the Customer Dashboard for a selected customer.

The Customer Dashboard is used to:

* View customer profile summary
* Review related operational history
* Track current activity context
* Access customer-linked workflows quickly

Use it as the central view for customer-specific operations before taking actions.

---

# 3. Why Customers Management Is Important

Strong customer data control helps your company:

* Reduce duplicate or invalid records
* Improve service and communication quality
* Keep job and billing data accurate
* Maintain audit-ready customer history
* Support reliable reporting and analytics

---

# 4. A. Viewing and Searching Customers

## What Information Is Typically Visible

* Customer name
* Customer code or reference ID
* Email and mobile number
* Company or account details (if applicable)
* Status (Active / Inactive)
* Last activity (if available)

---

## Why This Matters

This view allows administrators and operations teams to:

* Confirm whether a customer already exists
* Validate profile completeness
* Identify inactive or outdated records
* Support audit and support requests

---

# 5. B. Creating a New Customer

## When to Create a Customer

Create a customer when:

* A new customer starts engagement
* A lead is qualified and converted
* A company account requires onboarding

---

## Required Information

When creating a customer, provide:

* Full customer name
* Email address (if available)
* Mobile number
* Company name (for B2B accounts, if applicable)
* Address details
* Status (normally Active)

---

## Example Setup Flow

1. Open Customers page
2. Click Add Customer
3. Fill customer details
4. Validate contact information
5. Set status to Active
6. Save

---

## Post-Creation Validation

After creation, confirm:

✔ Customer appears in the list  
✔ Contact details are accurate  
✔ Duplicate record does not exist  
✔ Customer is selectable in operational workflows  
✔ Customer can be used in jobs and billing

---

# 6. C. Editing an Existing Customer

## When Should You Edit a Customer?

* Contact details change
* Name or profile data correction is required
* Account ownership changes
* Address or account information updates

---

## What Can Be Updated?

* Customer name
* Email and phone details
* Company/account details
* Address and notes
* Status and operational profile fields

After editing, verify updates are visible in list view and dependent modules.

---

# 7. D. Managing Customer Status (Active / Inactive)

## When to Deactivate a Customer

* Account is no longer active
* Duplicate was merged into another profile
* Compliance or legal restrictions require suspension

---

## Why Deactivate Instead of Delete?

Deactivation is preferred because it:

* Preserves historical jobs and invoices
* Keeps audit trail and references intact
* Prevents data loss in reports
* Blocks new transactions for inactive accounts

Deleting customers can break operational and financial records.

---

## Reactivating a Customer

If the account becomes active again:

* Change status to Active
* Validate operational access in related workflows
* Confirm customer can be used in new transactions

---

# 8. E. Operational Validation Checklist

After create/edit/status changes, confirm:

✔ Customer appears correctly in list  
✔ Customer profile fields are accurate  
✔ Active customers are available in workflows  
✔ Inactive customers are blocked from new operations  
✔ Related jobs, invoices, and reports remain consistent

---

# 9. Security & Operations Control Principles

For controlled customer administration:

* Limit create/edit access to authorized roles
* Review customer records periodically
* Merge or deactivate duplicates quickly
* Track major profile changes for audit
* Follow least-privilege access principles

Customers Management allows company teams to:
