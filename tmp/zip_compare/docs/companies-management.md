# Companies Management

## Company Setup & Control Guide

## Who Should Read This?

- Global Administrators
- Platform Operations Team
- Senior System Administrators

## What Is Companies Management?

Companies Management is where the platform controls and maintains all registered companies operating within the Global ERP system.

This includes:

- Creating new companies
- Updating company information
- Activating or deactivating companies
- Managing company-related user access

Each company may represent a different business entity operating under the global platform.

## Why This Is Important

Proper company management ensures:

- Clean organizational structure
- Accurate reporting by company
- Secure separation between businesses
- Controlled system access
- Reliable operational tracking

This is especially critical in a multi-company environment.

## A. Viewing Companies

Administrators can view and search all companies registered in the system.

### What You Can Do

- Search by company name
- Search by company code
- Filter by status (Active / Inactive)
- View detailed company information

### Why This Matters

It allows global administrators to:

- Monitor which companies are active
- Confirm company registration
- Review setup information
- Perform audits when needed

## B. Creating a New Company

### When Do You Create a Company?

A new company is created when:

- A new business entity joins the platform
- A new division is established
- A separate legal entity needs system isolation

### Required Information

When creating a company, you define:

- Display Name - The business name shown in the system
- Legal Name - Official registered name
- Company Code - Unique system identifier
- Country / City - Location information
- Primary Contact - Main responsible person
- Status - Normally set to Active

### Important Rule

The company code must be unique.
It acts as the system identifier for reporting and structure.

### Post-Creation Checklist

After creating a company, verify:

- The company appears in the global company list
- The company code is unique
- Default configurations are loaded properly
- Branch and user setup can proceed

## C. Editing an Existing Company

Company details may need updates when:

- Contact information changes
- Location changes
- Legal name updates
- Operational settings need adjustment

### What Can Be Updated?

- Display name
- Legal name
- Contact details
- Configuration settings

Always confirm changes carefully, as they may affect reporting and operational visibility.

## D. Password & Access Management

Important clarification:

A company itself does not have a login password.
Access is managed through company users, not the company record.

### Managing Company User Passwords

If a company user needs password reset:

1. Go to User Management
2. Filter users by the target company
3. Open the user profile
4. Reset the password
5. Confirm and communicate securely

Always enforce password policy and secure communication.

## E. Activating or Deactivating a Company

### When to Deactivate a Company

- Company stops operating
- Legal entity closes
- Temporary suspension
- Migration to another structure

### Important Guidance

Do NOT delete companies.
Always deactivate instead.

Deactivation:

- Preserves historical transactions
- Maintains audit trails
- Protects financial records
- Keeps reporting accurate

Before deactivation, confirm business impact with stakeholders.

## F. Operational Validation After Changes

After creating, editing, or deactivating a company, confirm:

- Company appears correctly in the global list
- Branch structure remains intact
- User-company mapping is correct
- Reporting structure is unaffected
- Inactive companies cannot create new transactions

Inactive companies should remain visible for historical reporting but blocked from new operational activity.

## Why This Matters in the Global ERP System

Strong company management ensures:

- Clear separation between business entities
- Accurate financial reporting per company
- Controlled branch operations
- Secure user access boundaries
- Clean multi-entity structure

Without proper company control, operational and financial data can become mixed or inconsistent.

## Simple Summary

Companies Management allows global administrators to:

- Register business entities
- Maintain accurate company information
- Control operational status
- Preserve historical data
- Maintain structured multi-company operations

It is a foundational control area of the Global ERP system.
